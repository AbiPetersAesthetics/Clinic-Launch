Proper Pasty

Every pasty shop in Cornwall, ranked by reviews, opening hours and how far you'd have to walk.

What matters to you

The Pasty Score is a weighted blend of three signals. Drag to suit — chasing a five-star pasty is a different holiday to wanting one in the next ten minutes.

Where the data comes from

This app ships with a built-in list of well-known Cornish makers so it works with no signal. Run a scan to pull live, surveyed data over the top.

Built-in Cornwall list Locations are town-level approximations. Opening hours are indicative patterns, not published hours — always shown with a dashed outline. No star ratings are invented.
OpenStreetMap — free, no key Sweeps every bakery and pasty shop mapped in Cornwall. Brings real coordinates and real published opening hours. Does not carry reviews.
Google Places — needs your own key The only source here that carries star ratings and review counts. Your key stays in this browser and is sent only to Google.
How to get a key (about three minutes, free tier)
  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and make a project.
  2. Enable Places API (New) under APIs & Services.
  3. Credentials → Create credentials → API key.
  4. Restrict it to the Places API. Google's free monthly allowance covers holiday use comfortably.
  5. Paste it above. It's saved in this browser only.

    Read this before you drive anywhere. Distances are straight-line, not road miles — a shop 3 km away across the Fal is a long way round. Opening hours shown with a dashed outline are indicative patterns for a Cornish bakery, not the shop's published times; ring ahead if it matters. Seasonal hours change constantly and pasty shops sell out.
