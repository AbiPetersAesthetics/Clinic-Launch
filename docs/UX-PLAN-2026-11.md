# Launch OS: the next version (UX plan, November 2026)

Written 15 November 2026. A plan only: nothing in the app, the data or anything live has been changed.

The brief, in the owner's words: "This needs to be elite now. Really intuitive, useful, needs to be
autonomous. I am high level, AI does everything else but exceptionally well."

What that means in practice, and what every decision below is tested against:

1. **Open it and be told.** The first screen says what matters today, in a sentence, then lists the
   few things that need David. He should never have to open four boards to work out whether
   anything is wrong.
2. **The AI does the legwork.** Scheduled runs collect the data, spot the problem and write the
   proposal, with the evidence attached.
3. **One tap to decide.** Approve, reject or ask, from a phone, with the left thumb.
4. **No figure without a source and a date.** If it is not measured, it says "not measured yet".
   If it is stale, it says so before it says anything else.
5. **It looks like the website.** Same ink, same type, same eyebrow, same 12px cards.

## 1. Critique of today's app

Read from the code on 15 November 2026: `src/components/layout.tsx`, `src/pages/home.tsx`,
`src/components/board-frame.tsx`, `src/App.tsx`, the board renderers in `api-server/src/boards/`
and the data files and run books in the clinic repo.

### 1.1 The home screen is a menu, not a brief

`pages/home.tsx` greets David ("Good morning. Where to?") and shows 13 tiles. Not one tile carries
a number, a status or a date. The Ads Board might have a lead that has not been answered for three
days, the Clinic Board might be two days stale, the card might be near its limit: Home looks
identical in every case. The owner has to open each board and read it to find out whether anything
needs him. That is the single largest gap against the brief.

### 1.2 The AI already writes conclusions, and the app hides them

The pathway check writes a plain-English `summary` three times a day ("Eleven leads and six booked
consultations... The landing page ad is the weak spot: 31 visits, no bookings yet."). The capacity
review writes a budget recommendation. Both are buried inside the Ads Board iframe, below the fold
on a phone. The most useful sentence the system produces never reaches the first screen.

### 1.3 There is nowhere for a recommendation to go

Runs recommend in emails, in board text and in `summary` strings. There is no list of open
recommendations, no record of what David approved or rejected, and no way to approve from the app.
"The capacity review recommends a budget step" currently ends with David reading an email and
opening a chat session. The loop the brief describes (AI proposes, owner taps, AI does it) does not
exist yet.

### 1.4 Figures that look fresher than they are

These are the findings that matter most, because the owner has said he hates misleading numbers
and none of them errors:

- **The Clinic Board claims a refresh that is not running.** `clinic-board.ts` prints "Refreshed
  daily at 07:30 from ANS" and the empty state says "the 07:30 refresh saves one each morning".
  `ops/clinic/REFRESH.md` says the `clinic-board-refresh` task could not be created and is waiting
  to be allowed, and it is not in the scheduled task list. The page promises a freshness it does
  not have. (It does show an age pill, which is good; the footer contradicts it.)
- **"Bank today" lives in the archive.** The Build Cash Board and the Owed page both tell David to
  "update Bank today on Money", and the link goes to `/financials`, which the sidebar files under
  Parked and archive as "Forecast Model" (a 4,646 line page). The single input that drives the
  master record is in the place labelled "kept for reference".
- **Boards stamp today's date on old inputs.** The Money Plan's eyebrow reads "live from Launch OS"
  with today's date, while its footnote says the patient figures run to 30 September 2026. The
  Build Cash Board does better (it shows "bank as at"), but neither shows the age of each input
  where the figure is read.
- **The compliance badge has no date.** The sidebar shows a percentage on Compliance & CQC,
  computed from the planning-era compliance list in `routes/dashboard.ts`. Nothing says when the
  list was last maintained, so the figure cannot be trusted or distrusted.
- **The layout still polls a retired model.** `layout.tsx` fetches the project dashboard every 15
  seconds and the optimisation analysis every 60 seconds, then discards both because the old strip
  and banner are switched off (`showOldStrip = false`, `showBanner = false`). That is roughly 400
  lines of dead UI and a steady background load for nothing.

### 1.5 Duplication

- Every board page shows its title twice: `PageHeader` in the app, then the board's own `<h1>` and
  eyebrow inside the iframe (`page-header.tsx` even carries a `compact` flag because "boards repeat
  it inside").
- Home and the sidebar list the same 13 destinations in slightly different groupings ("Build, until
  opening" on Home, "Build: retires after opening" in the sidebar).
- Ads figures appear on the Ads Board, the Clinic Board (ad-booked consultations), the Opening
  Board, the Ads Ideas board and the owner artifact `SMhMK18...`, all from one snapshot but each
  with its own wording.
- `/ads-pathway` redirects to `/ads-board`, while the pathway run book still describes an Ads
  Pathway page.

### 1.6 Buried or orphaned

- Seventeen archive pages sit one tap from the main nav, including Life Design and Franchise
  Model. They cost attention every time the drawer opens.
- "Export Full Report PDF" has a permanent place at the foot of the sidebar; it exports the
  planning-era model.
- Social and SEO are shown as greyed-out "WIP" items. A disabled item in primary navigation is a
  promise with no date; it trains the eye to skip the section.
- The scheduled runs that feed everything are only visible as a list inside the Ads Board. There is
  no page that answers "did the robots run last night, and did anything fail?"

### 1.7 Hard on a phone

- **Double scrolling.** `BoardFrame` gives the iframe a fixed height (`innerHeight - 190`, minimum
  700px), so on a phone the board scrolls inside a page that also scrolls. Thumb drags get caught
  by the wrong scroller.
- **A3 boards on a 375px screen.** The Build Cash Board is laid out as an A3 landscape sheet
  (1587px wide); it stacks, but the charts are dense and the controls rail sits above the content.
- **Navigation is two taps away.** The phone has a hamburger at top left (correctly placed for a
  left-handed owner, which is noted in the code), but every move between boards is open drawer,
  scroll, tap. The top bar's countdown is hidden below `md`, so the phone top bar shows only the
  logo.
- **Hover-only detail.** Board charts put their detail in SVG `<title>` hovers, which do nothing on
  touch.

### 1.8 Visual drift from the website

- The app shell uses 12px cards and pill nav items, like the website. The server boards use 2px
  radii, a grey page (`#F6F8FB`) and square buttons, so stepping into a board looks like leaving
  the app.
- The Build Cash and Cash Line charts use around fifteen series colours (`--c-start #2a78d6`,
  purples, pinks, oranges) that are not in the brand palette at all.
- The Money Plan page frame uses a warm cream ground (`#faf9f5`) and system fonts, which the
  app's own CSS comment rules out ("No warm cream ground").
- The eyebrow dot on Home is navy; on the website it is slate `#445B72`.
- The login screen uses square-ish `rounded-md` fields and a non-pill button.

### 1.9 What is already good, and must survive

- The brand tokens in `index.css` are close to the website and well commented.
- The boards are honest in their copy where they have been written recently: "Not measured yet"
  on the simulator, "Needs checking" for uncertain bookings, age pills on the Clinic Board.
- The pathway run book is excellent: cursors, a three-rung matching ladder, accuracy checks,
  read-only by rule. The new design builds on it rather than replacing it.
- Snapshot storage is already generic (`board_snapshots` keyed by board, pushed by
  `scripts/push-board.mjs`), so new data feeds cost almost nothing to add.

## 2. The target design

### 2.1 Concept: a chief of staff, with a narrower job than the name suggests

The suggested "AI chief of staff" model is right in spirit. Three refinements:

**a. Home is not a chat and not a dashboard; it is a brief.** The best executive tools lead with a
judgement, not a grid. Apple's Health Summary shows a few highlights and hides the rest behind
"Show all"; Stripe's home is a short set of charts, not the whole ledger. Launch OS Home should be
one verdict sentence, the three to five things that need David, and a single strip of vital signs.
Everything else is one tap away.

**b. The brief is composed by the server, not by an extra AI agent.** It is tempting to add a
"chief of staff" agent that writes the brief each morning. Do not. The agents that already run
(pathway check, Meta review, capacity review, SEO routines) each write their own conclusions and
proposals into the database. The server then ranks them with plain rules (red before amber, money
and patients before marketing, expiring before open-ended) and applies the freshness rules. This is
cheaper, never late, cannot hallucinate a figure, and still reads as written by a person because
each card's words come from the agent that did the work. An AI pass can be added later for the
one-line verdict if the rules feel mechanical.

**c. Four nouns, and only four.** Everything in the app is one of:

| Noun | What it is | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Brief** | Today's verdict, what needs you, vital signs | Home |
| **Proposal** | Something an agent wants to do, with evidence, awaiting a yes | The inbox (top of Home, and its own page) |
| **Board** | The detail of one area: verdict, KPIs, then everything | Money, Growth, Clinic |
| **Activity** | What the AI did, when, from what, and whether it worked | Activity page, and a line on Home |

If a screen is not one of these, it goes to More or the archive.

**Challenge to the suggested board list.** Five command boards (Money, Ads and Marketing, Clinic,
SEO, Team) is one too many for a phone tab bar and splits marketing in two. Recommended:

- **Money**: the Build Cash Board, the Cash Line, the Money Plan, cards, director's loan.
- **Growth**: Ads, Social and video, SEO and local listings, ideas and the market. One owner
  question ("are we getting patients, and what is it costing?") answered in one place, with tabs.
- **Clinic**: diary, takings, outcomes, people and capacity, compliance.
- **Team** is not built until there is a team. When the associate injector or the Patient
  Coordinator arrives, it becomes a tab inside Clinic, not a new area.

### 2.3 Navigation

**Desktop and tablet (768px and up): a slim sidebar.**

- Five items: Today, Money, Growth, Clinic, Inbox (with a count). Then a divider and Activity, More.
- Each area expands to its sub-pages only when it is the current area, so the sidebar never shows
  more than about twelve lines.
- Search sits at the top of the sidebar as a field that opens the command palette.
- The countdown pill ("Winchester opens in 23 days") moves into the Today verdict, where it means
  something, and leaves the top bar.

**Phone (below 768px): a bottom tab bar, ordered for the left thumb.**

```
+---------------------------------------------------+
| [Today 3] [Money] [Growth] [Clinic] [More]        |
+---------------------------------------------------+
  ^ easiest reach for a left thumb          hardest ^
```

- Today sits at bottom left, the easiest reach for a left thumb holding the phone, and carries the
  count of things needing David. More sits at bottom right, the least used.
- The tab bar is fixed, 64px tall plus the safe-area inset, labels always visible (icons alone fail
  on recognition).
- Inbox is reached from the Today tab (it is the top of the page) so it does not need its own slot.
- No hamburger. The top bar on phones holds the page title at left and a search button at left of
  centre, never in the top right corner, which is the hardest reach for a left hand.
- **Handedness setting** in More > Settings, defaulting to left. "Right" mirrors the order of the
  tab bar and the approve/reject buttons. This matters if Abi uses the app.

**Command palette (Ctrl+K or Cmd+K on desktop, the search button on phones).**

Built on the existing `components/ui/command.tsx` (cmdk). One box, three kinds of result, as Arc's
command bar did with tabs, history and actions:

- **Go to**: every board and sub-page ("cash line", "cards", "leads").
- **Find**: a lead by first name and initial, an ad by name, a plan line, a card entry.
- **Do**: "Update bank balance", "Approve all budget proposals" (shows a confirm list), "Run the
  pathway check now" (queues it), "Open the Ads Board full screen".

Keyboard on desktop, borrowed from Linear and Superhuman: `G` then `T`, `M`, `R` (gRowth), `C`
jumps to an area; in the inbox `J` and `K` move, `A` approves, `X` rejects, `Q` asks, `H` snoozes.
Every shortcut is also a visible button; keys are an accelerator, never the only way.

### 2.4 The shared board pattern

Every board, native or iframe, opens the same way. Three layers, always in this order:

```
1. VERDICT    one sentence, coloured dot, written by the run that produced the data
              "On track: 6 consultations booked from 11 leads; landing page ad is the weak spot."
              [Meta 2 h ago] [GHL 2 h ago] [ANS 2 h ago]

2. KPIs       three to five figures, each with a label, a value, a comparison and a source chip
              Leads 11 | Booked 6 | Cost per lead £7.12 | Cost per paying patient: not measured yet

3. DETAIL     everything else, in sections that collapse on phones; the full board for depth
```

Rules:

- **The verdict has three states**: on track (sage dot), watch (amber dot), act (red dot). A fourth,
  **stale** (hollow grey dot), replaces the verdict entirely when the data behind it is past its
  freshness limit: "Ads data is 14 hours old: the 08:00 check did not run. Figures below are as at
  yesterday 16:00."
- **No more than five KPIs.** Each KPI has a fixed anatomy: eyebrow label, value in the serif
  face, one comparison line ("up from 4 last week" or "target £30"), a source chip.
- **"Not measured yet" is a value, not an absence.** It is shown in the value slot in muted ink,
  with a reason ("needs 30 days after first consultations, from 2 Dec") and when it will first
  appear.
- **Every board says who keeps it current.** The footer names the run, its schedule and the time it
  last succeeded, linking to its Activity entry.
- **Clinic and VAT labels on money.** Following the existing rule, every revenue figure says
  Winchester, Bedhampton or both, and with VAT or kept.

### 2.5 Freshness and provenance

A single component, `<Fresh>`, renders every source chip, and a single server module decides what
"stale" means per source, so no page can invent its own rule.

| Source | Refreshed by | Stale after | Shown as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta spend and leads | Pathway check, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 | 9 hours | "Meta, 2 h ago" |
| GHL leads and replies | Pathway check | 9 hours | "GHL, 2 h ago" |
| ANS bookings and outcomes | Pathway check (outcomes at 08:00) | 26 hours | "ANS, 07:58" |
| ANS takings and diary | Clinic refresh, 07:30 (not yet allowed) | 26 hours | "ANS takings, 15 Nov 20:40" |
| Bank balance | Typed by David | 7 days | "Bank, typed 12 Nov" |
| Card register | Typed or imported | 31 days | "Cards, 10 Nov" |
| Search Console | GitHub Action, daily 03:17 UTC | 3 days (the existing rule) | "Search Console, today" |
| Semrush market data | One-off snapshot | Never refreshes | "Semrush snapshot, 19 Sep 2026" |
| Clarity | Pathway check | 26 hours | "Clarity, 12:04" |

Chip states: fresh (sage dot, ink text), ageing (amber dot), stale (hollow dot, the word "stale"),
failed (red dot, "last run failed, see Activity"). Tapping a chip opens a small sheet: the source,
the run that fetched it, the exact time, and the row in Activity.

A stale figure is never used in a verdict and never ranked into the brief as good news. A stale
source is itself a brief item ("Search Console data is 5 days old; the Action failed on Thursday").

### 2.6 The approval inbox

Every scheduled run that wants something done writes a **proposal** instead of an email paragraph.
A proposal is a card:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| . ADS  .  Capacity review, Mon 16 Nov 12:41                 |
| Raise the Meta budget from £25 to £30 a day                 |
|                                                             |
| Why: diary 38% booked for the next 14 days, cost per lead   |
| £7.12 over 11 leads, 6 booked. A £5 step adds about 5 leads |
| a week at today's cost.                     [Meta 1 h] [ANS] |
| Risk: cost per lead usually rises with spend.                |
| If approved: done at the next live session; re-checked      |
| first, cancelled if cost per lead is above £10.             |
| Expires: Thu 19 Nov 09:00                                   |
|                                                             |
| [ Approve ]   [ Reject ]   [ Ask ]          Show evidence > |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

Anatomy, in order: area eyebrow, the agent and time, the action as a verb phrase, why (with figures
and source chips), the risk, what happens on approval, the expiry, then the three buttons. Approve
is at bottom left for the left thumb, the largest target on the card.

**States.** Proposed, then one of: Approved (queued), Rejected, Asked (waiting for the agent's
answer), Snoozed, Expired. Approved then becomes Done (with the evidence of what was done) or
Cancelled at re-check (with the reason).

**Why approval queues rather than executes.** The app's safety rules block unattended spend,
publishing and deleting, and they are right to. A tap in the app is recorded as David's decision,
but an agent reading a database row later cannot treat that row as his consent for a spend change.
So:

1. David taps Approve. The card shows "Queued for the next live session" with a ten-second Undo
   (nothing has happened yet, so undo is free).
2. When David next opens a Claude session (or the app prompts him to, see 2.7), the session reads
   the queue and shows one confirmation in chat: "Three approved items: Meta budget £25 to £30,
   pause ad 'Glow 3', publish journal post. Go?" One "go" runs them all.
3. **Re-check before acting.** Each proposal carries the figure it was based on and a guard
   ("cancel if cost per lead is above £10"). The session re-reads the live figure first; if the
   guard trips, it cancels and writes a new proposal rather than acting on stale reasoning.
4. The result is written back to the card (Done, with the new budget read back from Meta) and to
   Activity.

Actions that need no consent at all (refreshing data, rebuilding a board, drafting copy) never
become proposals; they simply happen and appear in Activity.

**Reject** asks for an optional one-line reason, kept with the card so the agent learns ("rejected:
we hold spend until after the model days"). Agents read the last 30 days of rejections before
proposing the same thing again.

**Ask** opens a text field. The question is stored on the card; the next run of that agent answers
it on the card and the card returns to the inbox as unread. This is the "ask" from the brief without
building a chat product.

**Batching.** Several proposals of one kind (for example three ad pauses from the Meta review) group
into one card with "Approve all" and a list that expands. Superhuman's split inbox is the model:
sorted by kind so similar decisions are made together.

**Snooze**, borrowed from Linear: hide until a time; if the evidence changes before then, the card
comes back early.

### 2.7 Notifications

Fewer, better, and never a substitute for the brief.

| Trigger | Channel | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Morning brief, only if anything needs David | Email (house style, built with `seo/build-report-email.js`, sent to accounts@abipetersaesthetics.co.uk only) | 07:45 daily |
| Red alert: lead unanswered over 24 hours, spend anomaly, a run failed twice, site down | Push (phase 4); email until then | Immediately, 07:00 to 21:00 only |
| Proposal about to expire | Push; folded into the morning email until then | 12 hours before expiry |
| Weekly reports already sent (SEO Monday, Meta Mon and Thu, citations) | Unchanged; each gains a link to its cards in the inbox | As today |

Rules: no notification without a deep link to the exact card; quiet hours 21:00 to 07:00 except
"site down"; a notification that David ignores three times in a row is downgraded to the morning
email and a note says so in Activity. If nothing needs him, no email: silence means fine, and the
Today screen says "Nothing needs you" in so many words.

Web push needs a service worker and, on iPhone, the app added to the home screen (the manifest and
icons already exist in `public/`). Email covers everything until then.

### 2.8 Iframe boards: native, or stay

Today seven boards are server-rendered HTML shown in an iframe. Options:

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Keep all iframes** | No rework; the boards are rich, tested and match what the owner signed off | Double scroll, duplicate headers, off-brand styling, no deep links into sections, hover-only detail, Home cannot read their numbers |
| **Rebuild all native** | One design system, best on phones, deep links, proper accessibility | Weeks of work; the Build Cash Board and Money Plan carry engines and simulators that would have to be ported and re-verified against the owner's master record; high risk of a figure changing in translation |
| **Hybrid (recommended)** | Native verdict and KPIs on every board from day one; iframes kept for the deep, interactive detail and retired section by section where phones suffer most | Two rendering paths for a while |

The hybrid in detail:

1. **A summary contract per board.** Each renderer gains a sibling function that returns JSON:
   `{ verdict, state, kpis: [{label, value, compare, source, asOf}], alerts, sources }`. The server
   already has all the inputs; this is extraction, not new logic. Home and the board shell read it.
2. **The iframe drops its own header** when embedded (a `?embed=1` flag; `board-embed.ts` already
   exists for this kind of adjustment) and **reports its height** to the parent with
   `postMessage`, so the frame grows to fit and there is one scroll, not two.
3. **Shared board tokens.** One stylesheet injected into every server board: white page, 12px
   cards, pill buttons, slate eyebrow dot, focus rings. The boards then look like the app.
4. **Go native where phones suffer most, in this order:** Ads "Now" and the lead list (used daily,
   tabular, simple), the Clinic Board (small, already JSON-backed), then the Ads table of every ad.
   The Build Cash Board, the Money Plan simulator and the Cash Line stay iframes, behind a native
   summary, until there is a reason to move them; they are used on a laptop more than a phone.

### 2.9 Mobile layouts

- **One column, 16px gutters, nothing wider than the screen.** Tables become stacked rows (lead
  name, status pill, time) with a tap to open; no horizontal page scroll, matching the existing
  `overflow-x-hidden` rule.
- **KPIs as a 2 by 2 grid**, the fifth KPI (if any) dropped to the detail.
- **Charts get a tap target**: tapping a bar shows its value in a fixed line above the chart,
  replacing SVG hover titles.
- **Sticky verdict.** On board pages the verdict line shrinks into the top bar as you scroll, so the
  judgement stays in view.
- **Thumb zone.** Primary actions (Approve, Update bank balance, Open full board) sit in the lower
  half and on the left. Destructive or rare actions sit at the right.
- **Targets at least 44 by 44px**, with 8px between adjacent buttons so Approve and Reject cannot be
  hit together.
- **Pull to refresh** on Today re-reads the snapshots (it does not trigger runs).
- **Full screen board** stays for the Build Cash Board, rotated to landscape when the phone allows.

### 2.10 Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA.

- **Contrast.** Ink `#1F2A44` on white is about 14:1 and muted `#5C6577` about 6:1; both pass.
  Sage `#A7C0B6` (about 1.8:1) and sage deep `#7FA49A` (about 2.8:1) fail as text on white, so sage
  is used only for dots, fills, borders and focus rings, always with ink text beside it.
- **Status never by colour alone.** Every dot has a word ("On track", "Watch", "Act", "Stale").
  The red, amber and sage dots also differ in shape for the stale state (hollow).
- **Focus.** A 2px sage-deep ring with a 2px white offset on every interactive element, as the
  website's `--colour-focus`.
- **Keyboard.** Everything reachable by Tab in reading order; the command palette and inbox
  shortcuts are additions, never requirements.
- **Screen readers.** Each KPI is one labelled group ("Cost per lead, £7.12, Meta, 2 hours ago").
  Charts carry a text summary and a data table toggle. Iframes carry titles (they already do).
  Live regions announce "Approved, queued" and "Undone".
- **Motion.** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no animated counters.
- **Text size.** Body at 16px on phones (15px on desktop), honours browser zoom to 200% without
  loss.
- **Language.** `lang="en-GB"` everywhere, plain words, no jargon in verdicts ("cost per paying
  patient", not "CAC").

### 2.11 Empty, loading and error states

| State | What David sees | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **Loading** | Skeleton cards in the final layout; the previous values shown with "updating" if cached | A spinner over a blank page; a zero that later changes |
| **Nothing needs you** | "Nothing needs you. 4 runs since yesterday evening, all fine." with the vital signs below | An empty box |
| **Not measured yet** | The KPI slot reads "Not measured yet", the reason, and the date it first can be ("from 2 Jan, 30 days after the first consultations") | A dash, a zero, an estimate presented as a figure |
| **No data ever** (a new board) | What the board will show, which run will feed it, and whether that run exists yet | A greyed-out "WIP" nav item |
| **Stale** | The stale verdict (2.4), the figures still visible, each chip marked stale | Fresh-looking figures with an old date in the footer |
| **Source failed** | "Chrome was not connected at 12:00, so ANS was not read. GHL and Meta are current." with a link to Activity | A silent gap |
| **Board failed to build** | The server error in plain words, the last good version below it marked with its date, and a "tell Claude" button that copies the error | The raw stack in a pre block (today's `failed()` output and the app's `ErrorBoundary`) |

## 3. Autonomy

### 3.1 Principles

1. **Every agent owns one area and writes to three places**: its data snapshot, the proposals
   table, and the activity log. Nothing else needs to know how it works.
2. **Reads are free, writes are tiered.** See the matrix below.
3. **Silence is a failure, not a success.** Every run writes an activity row even when nothing
   changed. A missing row is detected and shown.
4. **Proposals expire.** A recommendation is valid for the data it was made on.
5. **The non-negotiable rules are enforced in code, not only in prompts.** The proposal writer
   rejects any proposal whose public copy contains a name on the POM blocklist (the journal system
   already has an interlock to reuse), a percentage discount, a guarantee word, or an en or em dash.

### 3.2 Permission tiers

| Tier | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **0. Alone, silently logged** | Read, compute, rebuild, draft | Refresh snapshots, rebuild boards, draft ad copy or a journal post, compute ROAS, run accuracy checks |
| **1. Alone, inside a written playbook, reported** | The owner has delegated it in writing, with guardrails | Google Ads keywords, negatives, bids and ad copy inside the £25 cap (owner, 10 Nov 2026, when the campaign is live); the daily health watch fixing broken links, invalid JSON-LD, dashes, stale dates; the weekly SEO change and at most one journal post (owner exceptions in the clinic CLAUDE.md) |
| **2. Proposal, then a yes, then a live session** | Spend, publish, delete, message, price | Any Meta or Google budget change or campaign pause; publishing ads, social posts, new pages; messages to leads or patients; deleting anything; ANS price changes (owner in chat only, never from a scheduled run) |
| **3. Never** | Blocked whatever is approved | Naming or pricing a POM in public copy; percentage discounts; guarantees; before-and-after without consent on file; unattended spend; handling credentials or creating keys |

### 3.3 The agents

Existing runs keep their names and run books; each gains the three writes. New ones are marked.

| Agent | Schedule | Owns | Alone (tier 0 or 1) | Proposes (tier 2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. Decisions for the owner

1. **How approvals become actions.** A tap records the decision; the action then runs in the next
   live Claude session after one "go" in chat, with a re-check first. This is required because the
   safety rules block unattended spend and an app record cannot stand in for a yes in chat. Is one
   daily "run the approved items" moment acceptable (for example prompted by the morning email)?
2. **Allow the daily clinic refresh** (07:30, read-only in ANS, instructions in
   `ops/clinic/REFRESH.md`). Without it the Clinic Board stays hand-refreshed and will be marked so.
3. **Switch off the `weekly-gsc-refresh` Chrome task**, which the clinic CLAUDE.md calls redundant
   now the daily GitHub Action refreshes Search Console.
4. **Chart colours on the Build Cash Board and Cash Line.** Replace the fifteen non-brand series
   colours with a ramp built from ink, slate, sage and sand? It changes the look of the master
   record, so it is your call.
5. **Bank balance source.** Keep typing it (with a weekly prompt and a stale mark after 7 days), or
   look at a read-only bank feed later. Until then it is shown as "typed on" a date, never as live.
6. **Who uses Launch OS.** David only, or Abi too? It decides whether handedness is a per-person
   setting and who may approve clinical or diary items.
7. **Google Ads autonomy wording.** The 10 Nov delegation lets scheduled runs change keywords, bids
   and copy inside the £25 cap without asking, while this brief says spend changes always need a
   yes. Confirm the line sits at the total daily budget and campaign status (tier 2), with
   keyword-level work tier 1.
8. **The archive.** Keep the 17 planning pages read-only under More, or retire some of them
   entirely. Retiring means deleting code, which needs your yes.
9. **Notifications.** Morning email to accounts@abipetersaesthetics.co.uk only (the existing rule),
   and push to your phone from phase 4, which on iPhone means adding Launch OS to the home screen.
10. **The name of the first tab.** "Today" (recommended, says what it is) or "Brief".
